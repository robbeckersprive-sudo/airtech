How this version works

Connect pipe segments with a suitable pipe coupling. The coupling and seal match the pipe size, operating conditions and the need for dismantling.

Follow the operating principle

  1. 01Positioning pipe ends

    The pipe ends to be connected are aligned according to the chosen connection.

  2. 02Enclosing the seal

    The seal fits against the contact surfaces around the pipe.

  3. 03Closing the coupling

    The clamping construction connects the parts according to the installation instructions of the version.

  4. 04Keeping the connection inspectable

    Sufficient space around the coupling allows inspection and dismantling.

The cross-section shows the principle. The final construction and process settings follow from the technical selection.

Frequently asked questions about pipe couplings.

What characterises this version?

A detachable connection between pipe sections. Connect pipe segments with a suitable pipe coupling. The coupling and seal match the pipe size, operating conditions and the need for dismantling.

Which pipe size is needed?

The actual outer diameter, wall build-up and pipe end shape are relevant. A nominal size alone is not always enough to choose a suitable coupling.

Where does this version fit in the process?

Installation, inspection and replacement of pipe segments. Describe the material, the required capacity, pressure or vacuum and process temperature.

What are the points of attention for maintenance?

Check connections, seals, wall thickness at loaded points and supports. Set the inspection frequency on the basis of operating hours, product load and the instructions for the version supplied.

Can this version be incorporated into an existing installation?

State wear-sensitive spots, cleaning requirements and the available space for supports and inspection. A photo or drawing helps to assess the connections and available space.

What information is needed for a targeted proposal?

Share your pipe route, material and required capacity. We help with diameter, connections and installation. State whether you are looking for a single component or also installation and connection. Let us know special product or cleaning requirements in advance.
