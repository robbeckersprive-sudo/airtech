Questions about
high vacuum.

Selecting the right blower?
Is this the same as vacuum conveying?

Both use negative pressure. Here the emphasis is on cleaning and targeted extraction. In product conveying, material capacity, feed and transfer to the next process step are central.

Can all connections be open at the same time?

Only if the installation has been designed for it. The agreed simultaneity helps determine the required flow rate and control.

Which vacuum source is suitable?

That follows from the operating points and operating conditions. An example component is not a final selection for every high-vacuum application.
