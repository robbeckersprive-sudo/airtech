01 / The starting point

A concrete question.
A fitting design.

The extension called for conveying over distance with attention to product hygiene. The existing vacuum blower was the starting point for the design.

Client
Driessen Food Extrusion
Material
Extrudates
Our role
Design and supply of conveying lines

02 / The solution

The parts.
The whole.

The first line carries extrudates from the cooler to a cyclone with dust filter. After size reduction and screening, the coarse fraction returns via the second line to the same receiving station.

  1. 01

    Dosing

    Extrudate from the cooler into the first line.

  2. 02

    Receive

    Separation in a central cyclone with filter.

  3. 03

    Processing

    Size reduction and screening into different fractions.

  4. 04

    Returning

    The coarse fraction back to the receiving station.
