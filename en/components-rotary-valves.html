Discuss your starting points

Frequently asked questions about rotary valves.

Does a valve always dose a fixed mass?

The rotor moves a volume. The mass depends on factors such as bulk density and filling of the rotor pockets. For accurate mass dosing, additional weighing or control may be needed.

When is a removable rotor useful?

If cleaning and inspection are needed regularly, an accessible rotor can simplify maintenance. We assess product properties, seals and available service space.

How does a valve connect to pneumatic conveying?

The valve is selected together with the conveying line and air balance. Product feed, pressure difference and leakage air are relevant starting points.

Is installation in an existing system possible?

We assess the connections, capacity and available installation space together with the existing process steps. Share a drawing or photo and describe what you want to improve.

Which data are needed for targeted advice?

Share your product, required capacity and pressure difference. We match the valve to the product flow and the connections. A drawing or photo of the current situation helps with the assessment. We discuss missing data during the analysis.

What do version and costs depend on?

Dimensions, choice of material, drive and required accessories determine the version. Installation, control and connection to the existing installation also count. A quotation follows on the basis of the agreed starting points and scope of supply.
