Frequently asked questions about filters.

When does a cartridge filter or a bag filter fit?

That depends on the dust, the load, temperature and cleaning requirements. We select the filter type and the medium together; the number of elements alone says little about suitability.

Is pre-separation needed?

With a high material load, a pre-separator can be part of the design. We assess whether this helps with the load and maintenance of the filter.

Can captured product be reused?

That depends on the product and the process requirements. Discuss the required return and any contamination before choosing the collection and discharge.

What does ATEX mean for the choice of filter?

With combustible dust, the inside of a filter is often a zone with an explosion risk. The dust data and your zone classification then determine the design, for example an antistatic filter medium, earthing and explosion venting or suppression. We match the design to the zone classification and the dust data you provide. Our article on ATEX and explosion safety explains which data is needed.

Is installation in an existing system possible?

We assess the connections, capacity and available installation space together with the existing process steps. Share a drawing or photo and describe what you want to improve.

Which data are needed for targeted advice?

Share your air flow, dust properties and required air quality. We think along about filtering, cleaning and discharge. A drawing or photo of the current situation helps with the assessment. We discuss missing data during the analysis.

What do version and costs depend on?

Dimensions, choice of material, drive and required accessories determine the version. Installation, control and connection to the existing installation also count. A quotation follows on the basis of the agreed starting points and scope of supply.
