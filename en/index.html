Industrial air technology & solids processing

In control of every flow.

Pneumatic conveying of powders and granules, from a single component to a complete process installation.

Clean air, nothing lost.

Filtration and dust extraction that remove fine dust from your process air and recover product.

Switch routes.

Diverters and valves route your bulk material through a single line to the right silo or mixer.

Let it swirl.

Cyclones separate product and conveying air by centrifugal force, without moving parts.

Every granule on time.

Rotary valves discharge powders and granules evenly and separate the pressure zones.

Flow patternMove over the pipe

Product moves slowly through the pipe as a bed or plug. Little air, little wear.

Product floats quickly in the air stream. A lot of air, simple and flexible.

Filter operation

Dust-laden air flows through the cartridges. The dust stays behind as a cake on the filter medium.

An air pulse blows the cake off the cartridges. The product falls back into the cone.

Particle size

Coarse particles are flung against the wall and settle through the cone to the outlet.

Some of the finest particles follow the air upwards through the vortex finder. That is why a filter often follows.

Speed

Each pocket carries a fixed volume. A low speed gives a calm, small flow.

The same pockets, more often per minute: the dosing capacity rises with the speed.

  1. 01

    Analysis

    Analysis of your production process

    We map out your process and the required capacity. For pneumatic conveying we look at the material and the pipe route. For industrial dust extraction we investigate where dust is released and which extraction points are needed.

    What you get

    • Overview of process data and bottlenecks
    • Capacity and integration requirements
    • Advice on the solution direction

    In this phase

    Together we determine which process data are needed to work out the design.

  2. 02

    Engineering

    Engineering of the process installation

    We design an installation for your production environment. For conveying powders or granules, we work out air quantity, pressure and pipe diameter. For dust extraction, we match the fan, the pipework and the filtration to each other.

    What you get

    • Process and installation design
    • Component selection and pipe route
    • Technical drawings for construction and installation

    In this phase

    We match the connections to your production line and keep space free for inspection and maintenance.

    Discuss the design of your installation
  3. 03

    Realisation

    Construction and installation

    We manufacture or supply the parts according to the design and install the process installation on site. In doing so, we connect the pipework and the selected components to the new or existing production line.

    What you get

    • Installed pipework and components
    • Connections to the production line
    • Installation check

    In this phase

    We align the installation schedule with the available space and your production planning.

  4. 04

    Commissioning

    Commissioning and handover

    We start up the installation, set the operating parameters and test performance against the agreed requirements. Depending on the application, we check for example the conveying capacity or the performance of the dust extraction.

    What you get

    • Adjusted and tested installation
    • Feedback on the test results
    • Handover to your team

    In this phase

    At handover, we go through operation and the points of attention for daily use with your team.

Do you also supply individual components?

Yes. Airtechnic Solutions supplies individual components and complete process installations for industrial air technology and bulk processing. Think of cyclones, dust filters, blowers, fans and rotary valves. We help you select them on the basis of your product, the required capacity and the connection to your installation.

Can you improve our existing installation?

Yes. We investigate how your existing installation for pneumatic conveying, dust extraction or bulk processing can be improved. We look at bottlenecks in capacity, energy consumption, wear and maintenance. Based on the process data, we advise on changes to the pipework, replacement of components or extension of the system.

What information is needed for an initial conversation?

Tell us which product you process, what you want to improve and which capacity you need. For pneumatic conveying, the conveying distance, the height difference and the properties of the powder or granulate are also relevant. For dust extraction, information about the dust sources and extraction points helps. Photos, drawings and data of your current installation give extra insight. Together we determine which additional data are needed.

Which industries do you work for?

We supply solutions for industries including food, animal feed, chemicals, recycling and wood processing. Our industrial air technology and solids processing are used to extract and filter dust, to convey powders and granules pneumatically and to separate and dose bulk materials. We tailor the design to the product and the production environment.

How do you choose the right pneumatic conveying system?

The choice depends on the product, the required capacity, the conveying distance and the available space. We look at factors such as particle size, bulk density and sensitivity to product damage. With these data we determine whether pressure or vacuum conveying is suitable and match the air flow, the pipework and the components to each other.

Do you design custom process installations?

Yes. We design installations for industrial air technology and solids processing based on your process and production environment. We combine suitable components with custom work in the pipe route, the layout and the connections. This way we can fit a new installation or an extension into your production line.

Do you also provide maintenance and overhaul?

Yes. Airtechnic Solutions provides maintenance, overhaul and optimisation of existing installations. We look at the technical condition and the operating conditions to determine which work is needed. Preventive maintenance helps to detect wear in good time and supports the reliability of your pneumatic conveying, dust extraction or bulk processing.

How does a project run from design to handover?

We start with an analysis of your process, product and capacity requirements. We then work out the installation design and component selection. After agreement, supply and installation follow. During commissioning we adjust the installation, check its operation against the agreed requirements and hand the installation over to your team.
