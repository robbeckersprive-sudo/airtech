What matters
in your industry?

Animal feed and pet food: the issues we encounter most often here, and what they mean for the design of the installation.

  1. 01

    Many raw materials, many destinations

    One receiving point often has to serve several silos or mixers.

    For the design We design diverter valves, routing logic and receiving stations as one system, with clear routes.

  2. 02

    Fatty, moist and sticky

    Such products flow poorly from silos and big bags and stick to walls.

    For the design Outlet geometry, discharge aids and the choice of rotary valve follow from the actual flow behaviour.

  3. 03

    Cross-contamination

    Residues of medicated or specific raw materials must not end up in the next batch.

    For the design We include complete emptying, cleanability and the sequence of routes in the design.

  4. 04

    Dust and explosion safety

    Tipping and receiving release fine, often combustible dust.

    For the design Extraction at source and filters matched to the dust data and your zone classification.

How many destinations can one conveying line serve?

That depends on the capacity per route and how often switching takes place. With diverter valves, one receiving point can serve several silos or receivers. In the animal feed project, three diverter valves distribute the product over four receiving stations.

Why does a high-fat product flow poorly from a silo or big bag?

Fat and moisture make a product cohesive: it forms bridges or sticks to the wall. We match the outlet geometry, discharge aids and the discharge rotary valve to the actual flow behaviour of your product.

How do you limit the risk of cross-contamination?

By designing routes so that they empty well, making residues visible and accessible, and taking the sequence of batches into account. Specify which raw materials must remain strictly separate.

Does GMP+ play a role in the design?

GMP+ is a certification scheme for safe animal feed, managed by GMP+ International, which many companies in the industry work with. The requirements apply to your company. Share the requirements that apply to your site and we will include them in the design basis for cleanability and residues.

Is dust from animal feed raw materials explosive?

Many organic raw materials can explode in a dust cloud. Whether this applies to your product is shown by the dust data. Together with your zone classification, these determine the design of filters and rotary valves.
